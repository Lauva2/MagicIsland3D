# 主菜单背景

使用内置 image_gen 生成，保存到 `Assets/Art/Textures/UI/MainMenuBackground.png`。
由 `MainMenuUI` 的 Background Image 引用，作为 AA 预制体的依赖一起加载。
背景按比例铺满，超出画布的部分裁切，不拉伸。

## 生成提示词

+ Use case: stylized-3d game environment illustration.
 Asset type: 16:9 landscape main menu background for Magic Island, a cozy 3D farming life game. Aim for 2048x1152 landscape.
 Scene: a small lush island farm in a calm turquoise sea, gentle elevated oblique view, charming cream plaster farmhouse with warm terracotta roof on the RIGHT half, neat vegetable plots, wooden fences, orchard, curving sandy footpath, flowers, distant soft islands and pale sky. A few tiny magical glimmers in the garden, restrained and welcoming.
 Style: polished stylized 3D diorama, rounded simple forms, tactile soft matte materials, appealing hand-crafted game environment, rich detail without visual clutter.
 Composition: reserve the LEFT THIRD as quiet low-detail soft sage meadow and pale sky with gentle tonal contrast, suitable for a title and menu overlay. Place farmhouse and main garden details center-right. Broad panorama filling frame, no borders.
 Lighting and palette: warm late-afternoon sun, soft shadows, honey gold, cream ivory, sage and moss green, muted teal water. Peaceful inviting mood.
 Constraints: background artwork only. No words, no letters, no logos, no buttons, no menu, no watermark, no characters. Original environment, not copying any existing game art.
